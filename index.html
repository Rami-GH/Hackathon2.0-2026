COMMUNITY INCIDENT REPORTING

Something doesn't feel right?

Help protect the community by sharing a suspicious message or digital service issue.

Tell us what happened

This prototype keeps your report in this browser only. Don't include passwords or one-time codes.

🔒 Your information should be handled securely. In a real service, this report would be sent through an authenticated government channel. This demo does not transmit or store your report.

If you think your account is at risk

  • Change your password from the official website or app.
  • Turn on multi-factor authentication if available.
  • Contact the service provider through a verified channel.
  • If money may be at risk, contact your bank promptly.

Keep yourself safe

  • Never share a one-time passcode with anyone.
  • Don't click links in suspicious messages.
  • Save a screenshot if it helps explain what happened.
