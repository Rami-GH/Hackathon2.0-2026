If you think you may be affected

Use a government app or website you find independently to confirm a payment request. Do not use contact details or links in the suspicious message.

  • If you shared payment details, contact your bank using a trusted channel.
  • If you shared a password or code, contact the relevant service through its official app or site.
  • Keep the original message if you need to show it to an authorized support team.

Aman cannot submit a real report or provide emergency assistance.

Know what this check can tell you

This is a basic local inspection, not a full antivirus engine. It can catch some warning signs, but a clean result does not guarantee a file is safe. Keep your device security software on and only open files from sources you trust.

Know what this check can tell you

This is a text-only clue check. It cannot verify who sent a message, authenticate a government service, or determine whether a fine is real. Confirm payment requests by opening the official app or website yourself.
